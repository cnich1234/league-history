# Week 1 Preview: Everybody's 0-0 and Everybody's Lying to Themselves

Eighteen years of receipts get wiped clean for exactly one week. Every manager in this chat currently believes this is the year they finally figure it out, which is adorable, because most of you have never figured out anything. Draft-day optimism is the purest form of self-delusion known to man, and this league has been mainlining it since 2008. Let's get to the matchups before reality sets back in.

## Etienne Ass (Chris R) vs I Cashed @ WSOP x8 (Ernie)
Chris R shows up at 6-9 for his career and sitting 3 games under .500, which tracks for a guy who finished 9th last season and missed the playoffs entirely. He's also 0-2 all-time against Ernie, and the last meeting wasn't close — a 72.5-point beatdown with Ernie's side dropping nearly 200. Ernie, for his part, is 19 games under .500 for his career despite that blowout, so consistency is not the brand here either. Somehow Ernie still made the playoffs last year at 10-5 and only finished 4th, which is its own kind of incompetence. Give this one to Ernie on history alone — Chris R hasn't beaten this man once, and Travis Etienne playing for the Saints isn't going to be the thing that changes that.

## Gronkey Punch (Mike B) vs Devin (Devin)
Mike B has a ring from 2020 and absolutely nothing since, cratering to 4-11 and a 10th-place finish last year while sitting 17 games under .500 for his career. Devin's title came in 2008 — the actual first season this league existed — and he's been coasting on that banner for seventeen years while somehow staying 6 games over .500 for his career, which is more than most people in this room can say. The all-time series has been fought 29 times and Devin's up 18-11, including a 2010 disaster where he won 169-71 and probably still brings it up unprompted. Devin currently holds a one-game win streak in the head-to-head, for whatever that's worth against a guy running Drake Maye and four separate questionable tags into Week 1. Devin wins this, mostly because Mike B's roster reads like a training room report.

## JSN Derulo (Kevin) vs Bud Light from Target (Brandon)
Kevin's sitting on two championships and a career mark 44 games over .500, which is the best number anyone in this preview is going to post, so enjoy it, Kevin, it's the only nice thing being said all day. Brandon's got one ring of his own from 2018 but is barely above water for his career at plus-2, and somehow finished 9-6 last year and still missed the playoffs entirely — the exact same record Kevin posted while landing a 2nd-place finish, which tells you everything about how Brandon's season actually went. Brandon technically leads the all-time series 13-12 across 25 meetings, so he's got bragging rights on paper. But Kevin's riding a 2-game win streak in the matchup and owns the single ugliest blowout in its history, a 189.3-117.3 massacre in 2021. History says Brandon, the streak says Kevin — go with the guy who's actually won recently.

## Deebo My Lemons (Chad) vs Mike R
Chad's lone championship came in 2015, a decade of nothing since, and he's dragging a career mark 15 games under .500 into another season, fresh off a 4-11 finish that landed him 8th. Mike R has three rings (2016, 2019, 2024) and sits 20 games over .500 for his career, but even he stumbled to 5-10 and a 5th-place finish last year, so the trophy case didn't save him from a rough season either. The all-time series has Mike R up 16-12 across 28 meetings, and he's carrying a current 1-game streak plus the single biggest blowout in this matchup's history — a 188.6-75.4 mauling in 2022 that Chad has presumably tried to forget. Chad's leaning on Josh Allen and a rookie tight end to change his luck. It won't — Mike R takes this one on volume of championships and prior trauma inflicted.

## Token Effort (Chris N) vs Seal Team Nix (Austin)
This is as close to a title-game rematch as Week 1 gets on paper. Chris N has two rings of his own (2010, 2014) and sits 8 games over .500 for his career, coming off a 9-6, 3rd-place playoff season — respectable, unspectacular, very on-brand. Austin is the actual reigning champion, five rings total now with 2025 being the newest addition, and yet his career record sits 10 games under .500 — a guy who is somehow historically mediocre and currently untouchable at the same time. Chris N leads the all-time series 16-12 across 28 meetings, but Austin's taken the last two straight, so recent form says the ring matters more than the receipts. Pick Austin — insufferable 5-time champion behavior, but until someone actually beats him again, that's just where we are.

## Lock of the Week
Ernie's stat sheet says DK Metcalf and DJ Moore both play through their questionable tags, go off, Ernie wins big — and somehow still ends up looking up at three other teams in the standings come December, because that 4th-place finish on a 10-5 record last year wasn't a fluke, it's a personality trait.
