# Week 1 Recap: Kevin Scores 191, Devin Steals One on Monday Night, and Chris R Trades His Way Into Last

One week in and the preview already looks smarter than most of you. Five games, one blowout, one coin flip decided by a Denver running back's eighth and final carry, and a Monday night comeback that had Mike B refreshing the app like it owed him money. Let's go around the room.

## JSN Derulo (Kevin) 191.66, Bud Light from Target (Brandon) 147.40
Kevin posted the high score of the week and it wasn't close. Christian Watson went for 33.7, Justin Jefferson 31.2, Jaxon Smith-Njigba 27.2, Jonathan Taylor 25.1, and Kevin left nothing on the bench, which the Trophy Room calls a perfect lineup and the rest of us call showing off. Brandon, meanwhile, scored 147.4, fourth-highest in the league this week and more than two of the five winners. Dallas Goedert had 23.7, Breece Hall 21.8, Nico Collins 21.2, and none of it mattered because he drew the one guy who went nuclear. Brandon is officially the unluckiest man in the league after seven days. Two championships, forty-four games over .500, and Kevin still finds a way to make it personal.

## I Cashed @ WSOP x8 (Ernie) 172.16, Etienne Ass (Chris R) 102.96
Sixty-nine point beatdown. Ernie was projected to lose this game by four and won it by sixty-nine, which is the single biggest beat-the-spread in the league this week and probably the year. Caleb Williams threw for 41.26 in that 59-37 Chicago circus in Carolina, Derrick Henry ran for 37.3, Bijan added 31.3, and Ernie did all of it with Kyle Pitts and the Chargers defense combining for exactly zero. Chris R scored 102.96, the lowest total in the league, with Jake Ferguson at 2.6 and George Pickens at 5.8 while Lamar Jackson's 28.96 went entirely to waste. Historically Chris R had never beaten Ernie. That is still true.

## Token Effort (Chris) 155.42, Seal Team Nix (Austin) 119.54
The reigning champion opened his title defense with Ja'Marr Chase scoring 3.2 and Bo Nix scoring 7.44, and D'Andre Swift's 34.4 was the only reason this wasn't a bloodbath. Chris got 35.6 and 156 rushing yards from Gibbs, 29.2 and 182 receiving yards from Olave, 30.72 from Hurts, and survived the Houston Texans posting negative five in a 36-31 game against Buffalo. You can start a defense that costs you points and still win by 36 when the other guy's first-round receiver has two catches. Austin leads the recent head-to-head 2-0. Make that 2-1.

## Deebo My Lemons (Chad) 139.16, Mike R 138.84
Thirty-two hundredths of a point. Mike R had Ashton Jeanty at 34.7, Amon-Ra at 28.7, Dalton Kincaid at 19, and was done by Sunday afternoon at 138.84 with everyone home. Chad went into Monday night at 135.56 with one player left: J.K. Dobbins, a Denver running back sharing a backfield, in a game the Chiefs won 31-10. Chad needed 3.29. Dobbins got 3.6. Josh Allen's 41.66 was the top player performance in the league this week, and it was worth exactly as much as the 3.6 that came after it. Mike R also had A.J. Brown and Davante Adams combine for 11.2, so there was plenty of room to find a third of a point. He didn't.

## Devin 135.40, Gronkey Punch (Mike B) 130.02
Mike B went into Monday night at 128.82 with one starter left, Jaylen Waddle, who caught one ball for 1.2 and finished him at 130.02. Zay Flowers 27, Trey McBride 24.5, Chuba Hubbard 23.7, and Drake Maye's 11.82 from a Wednesday game nobody remembered was happening. Devin sat at 89.2 with Kenneth Walker and Travis Kelce left, needing roughly 40 from two Chiefs. Matthew Stafford had already given him 4.1, which is not a typo.

Here is how Monday went, minute by minute, because the app was watching:

- **Halftime**: Walker had 12.5, Kelce 1.6, Devin at 103.3, still down 27.
- **6:59 PM**: Walker rips a 60-yard touchdown run and jumps from 12.5 to 26.5 in one play. Devin at 117.3.
- **7:40 PM**: a second Walker touchdown, this one a two-yard catch, 26.7 to 33.9, and Devin crosses 130 with about a quarter of the game left.
- **Final**: Walker 36.1 on 173 rushing yards, Kelce 10.1, Devin 135.4.

Devin won with the lowest winning score in the league, got the Ugly Win trophy for it, and now leads the all-time series 19-11. Mike B outscored Austin and Chris R and still lost. He and Brandon should talk.

## Trade of the Week
Chris R proposed a trade on Thursday afternoon, about an hour before the Rams game kicked off, and it went through Friday morning: Matthew Golden to Ernie for Josh Jacobs. Then they played each other. Golden scored 15.5. Jacobs scored zero. Neither one was in a starting lineup, so the trade decided nothing on the scoreboard, but the optics of shipping the productive Packer to the man who then beat you by 69 are hard to improve on. Both managers collect the Wheeler Dealer trophy for the effort. Chris R's other trophies this week were Lowest Score and Worst Tight End, so at least the trade one was on purpose.
